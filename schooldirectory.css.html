HI, I'M VALERIE

I design every uniform, answer every call, and build every page of this site myself — self-taught, start to finish. CHEERGEAR started as a passion for squads who deserve to look as good as they perform, and it's grown into something I'm incredibly proud of.

I'm based in Kansas City, MO — and I work with coaches all over the country. If you have a vision for your squad, I'll make it happen. Every color. Every detail. Every time.

Outside of uniforms, I'm a musical artist — guitar, drums, piano, and mandolin. I'm also a contortionist who is constantly pushing past my own limits, physically and creatively. Whether I'm on the mat or behind a screen building this site, I bring the same drive.
